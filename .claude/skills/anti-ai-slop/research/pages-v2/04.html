Between sets

What happens in the 90 seconds after the bar goes down

  1. Tap 1

    Weight and reps are pre-filled from your previous set. If you did the same thing, you change nothing.

  2. Tap 2

    Log set. It saves offline and starts the rest countdown in the same press. The lock-screen timer keeps running with the phone in your pocket.

  3. Rest ends

    One haptic pulse, no sound unless you turn it on. The next set is already waiting with its target.

  4. Running

    Switch to a run and the same screen shows pace, distance and the heart-rate zone. Intervals use the same rest timer.

Heart-rate zones

Five zones, colored like the plates on the bar

Zone edges are percentages of your max heart rate, so you can see at a glance whether the set you just finished was hard or the walk back to the rack was. Enter your own max or let FitPulse use 220 minus your age until you do.

  1. 1Recovery50 to 60% of max
  2. 2Easy aerobic60 to 70% of max
  3. 3Tempo70 to 80% of max
  4. 4Threshold80 to 90% of max
  5. 5Maximum90 to 100% of max

Zones are a training guide, not medical advice.
