Each person holds five open tasks. The sixth waits.

Taskflow is one sheet per week for teams of three to twelve: a task has exactly one owner, and the app refuses to give anyone a sixth open task until they close one.

Start this week's sheet Takes one form below. No import step.

Three rules a shared to-do list never enforces

01

One owner per task

A task cannot be assigned to "the team" or to two people. If you want two people on it, split it into two tasks with two names and two due days.

02

Five open tasks, then it says no

The cap is a setting from 3 to 9, default 5. Past it, the add button turns into close one first. Overload shows up on Monday, not in a retro three weeks later.

03

The sheet ends on Friday

Open tasks do not roll over silently. Each one is either closed, dropped, or carried by its owner, who has to retype the due day.

Friday at 16:00, the sheet asks each owner one thing

For every task still open, the owner picks close, drop or carry. Carried tasks go to next week's sheet under the same name, with a counter so a task carried three times is visible to everyone.

There is no rollover bucket, no "backlog" tab, and no filter that hides what you did not finish.
