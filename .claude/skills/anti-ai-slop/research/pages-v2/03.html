Four passes, run one at a time.

Each pass looks for one kind of problem, so a tightening edit never arrives disguised as a grammar fix.

The four editing passes
PassLooks forLeaves alone
TightenHedges, stacked prepositions, passive constructions that hide the actor.Sentence length you chose on purpose. Short and long are both allowed.
ClarifyPronouns with two possible referents, claims with no object, undefined terms.Jargon your audience uses. You can list it once.
MatchDrift from your style sheet: serial comma, spelling variant, how you write product names.Anything your style sheet does not mention.
ProofTypos, doubled words, mismatched quotes and brackets.Dialect, slang, intentional misspellings in quoted text.

Where Nimbus stops.

  • It does not change a sentence without saying why.

    An edit with no reason attached is not shown. If the reason is weak, you will see that it is weak, and reject it.

  • It does not touch quoted text.

    Anything inside quotation marks or a block quote is read but never edited. A quote is someone else's wording.

  • It does not flatten your register.

    A formal letter stays formal and a newsletter stays chatty. The Match pass learns from the sentences you reject.

  • It does not decide facts.

    When a claim is vague, Nimbus leaves a query in the margin, like the one above, and waits for you.
