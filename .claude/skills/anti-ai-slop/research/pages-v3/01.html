On a Taskflow board a task hangs on one person. If it has no owner, you see an empty outline where it should be.

What an empty outline tells you

Board above is a sample, with tasks owned by roles.

Each outline answers who has it and whether it is still open

Solid outline
One person owns it. Their role and the due day sit on the task, so nobody has to ask in a thread.
Dashed yellow outline
No owner. The task stays on the board, loud, until someone takes the hook.
Faded, struck through
Finished. It stays visible for the rest of the day so the team can see what cleared.
