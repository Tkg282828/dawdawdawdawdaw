Each stage hands the next one a drawing it can build from

  1. Survey and light study

    Levels, party walls, the neighbour’s windows and where the sun falls across them.

  2. Concept sections

    Two or three cuts through the building that test height against the study.

  3. Planning drawings

    The set the council reads, drawn only after the sections hold.

  4. Construction drawings

    Junctions, build-ups and openings a builder can price and a surveyor can check.

  5. On site

    Visits at the points where the drawings meet the ground.
