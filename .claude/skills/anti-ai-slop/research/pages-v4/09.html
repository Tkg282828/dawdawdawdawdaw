Where the key is made and where it stays

The key is made and kept on your device

Your master password never travels. Vaultix turns it into a key locally, encrypts the vault with that key, and uploads only the result.

Your device

Master password plus a random salt go through Argon2id to make the vault key. The vault is sealed with AES-256-GCM.

master password + salt > vault key

Vaultix servers

Sync stores an encrypted blob and its version. There is no key on the server, so a database leak exposes ciphertext only.

Losing the master password

Can Vaultix reset it?

No. The server has no copy of the key, so a reset would mean decrypting your vault. You get a recovery key at sign-up to print or store offline.

What happens if the service goes down?

Each device keeps an encrypted local copy, so unlocking and autofill keep working offline. Sync resumes when the connection returns.

Can I export my logins?

Yes, as a CSV or an encrypted file, from any desktop app or the web vault.
