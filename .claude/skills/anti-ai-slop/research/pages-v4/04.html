What FitPulse logs

Strength
Exercise, weight, reps and rest time for every set, with last session's numbers beside the current one.
Runs and rides
Distance, pace and route from your phone's GPS.
Heart rate
Beats per minute and time spent in each zone, read from a connected sensor.
Body weight
Entered by you whenever you weigh in, and charted by week.
Consistency
Workouts completed against your weekly plan, and how many weeks in a row you hit it.

Weights in kilograms or pounds, distances in kilometres or miles.

Heart rate zones

Zone boundaries are percentages of your maximum heart rate, so the same session reads differently for a beginner and a marathoner.

Zone 1Recovery, 50-60% of max
Zone 2Fat burn, 60-70% of max
Zone 3Aerobic, 70-80% of max
Zone 4Threshold, 80-90% of max
Zone 5Maximum, 90-100% of max
