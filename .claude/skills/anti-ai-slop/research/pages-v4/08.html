Each plant gets a schedule built from its species and your light, then a thread you can write to.

Cancelling and replacements

Skip a month or cancel from your account. Changes made before Friday 5 pm apply to the next box, and nothing else is charged.

A plant that arrives damaged

Send a photo within 7 days and we replace it at no cost.
