A single studio from brief to handover

New houses
Site study, planning, structure and detailing for single dwellings, drawn by the architect who will visit the site.
Extensions and renovations
Measured surveys of the existing building, so the new work meets old walls where they really are.
Schools and public buildings
Briefing with the people who will use the building, then procurement drawings and contract administration.
Planning applications
Drawings, design statements and negotiation with the planning officer, also for projects we did not design.
