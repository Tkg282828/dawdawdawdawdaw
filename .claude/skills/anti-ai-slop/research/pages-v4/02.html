Roast days and cutoffs

We only roast what has been ordered, so every bag has a roast date you can check on the label.

  1. Sunday, 23:59

    Order cutoff for the week's roast.

  2. Tuesday

    Small-batch roast, cupped before anything is bagged.

  3. Wednesday

    Bagged with the roast date on the label and handed to the courier.

  4. Thursday to Friday

    Delivery. Beans taste best from the second day after roasting, so a bag that arrives now is nearly ready to brew.
